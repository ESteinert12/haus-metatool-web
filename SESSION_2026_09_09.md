## /api/b2/create-stems — how it works
    ?dryRun=1 (default) | ?dryRun=0 | ?cohort=NAME | ?limit=N
- Titles with NO mix_stems rows; pages the bucket; matches on the sku_root in
  the PATH (present in 87.3% of keys, 30,406 distinct SKUs).
- SKIPS the `music/` tree entirely and anything under 1KB.
- stem_name from `_detectStemName` — PORTED VERBATIM from index.html. If you
  change one, change both.
- Groups by stem_name AND EXTENSION. A song legitimately has FULL.wav and
  FULL.mp3; keying on stem_name alone called 37,338 normal pairs "ambiguous".
- Genuine ambiguity = same stem, same extension, different folder. REPORTED,
  never guessed. Writes a full JSON report every run.
