   real job: essentially the whole catalogue.
   The Mac has ffmpeg at /usr/local/bin/ffmpeg. Claude's VM cannot reach B2,
   so a bulk detector runs on the Mac or against files the server streams.
   Whatever it produces is an ESTIMATE. Store it, but FLAG IT AS DERIVED so a
   human can see which numbers were machine-guessed, and never silently mix
   derived values with ones a person entered. Tempo detection is reliable on
   a steady groove and unreliable on rubato, stings and one-shots — expect the
   specialty families to need a human eye.
